
📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

🚀 Dashboard Overview
The Super Store Sales Dashboard helps users visualize and understand sales and profit trends to support strategic business decisions. It presents key performance
indicators and visual insights in an intuitive and interactive format.

- **Sales by Segment**  
  Analyze sales performance across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

- **Sales by Region**  
  Compare sales trends across East, West, Central, and South regions.

- **Sales by Payment Method**  
  Understand customer preferences based on different payment modes.

- **Sales & Profit by State**  
  Drill down into state-level performance with profit visualization.

- **Sales by Category & Sub-Category**  
  Dive deeper into product-level sales for better inventory and marketing decisions.

- **Profit Insights**  
  Identify high and low-profit areas to make data-driven improvements.

 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** (for dataset)
- **Super Store Sample Dataset** (can be customized or replaced)

 📈 Use Cases

- Business performance monitoring
- Regional and product-level sales tracking
- Profitability analysis
- Data storytelling and presentation
